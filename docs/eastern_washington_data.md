| Shallow-station candidate inventory; required to select and locate validation sensors | NRCS, NOAA USCRN and USBR AgriMet station metadata; catalog snapshot 2026-10-15 UTC, with raw catalog responses in `data/station_catalogs/2026-10-15/` | Tracked snapshot: [`data/reference/eastern_washington/soil_moisture_stations.json`](../data/reference/eastern_washington/soil_moisture_stations.json) | EPSG:4326 point coordinates; nominal sensor depths 5–5.08 cm for the five selected sensors; inventory spans the study window | The [station-map workflow](../scripts/build_station_map.py) refreshes the source catalog and map. | Shallow soil-moisture observations; required for station validation | NRCS SCAN/SNOTEL, NOAA USCRN and USBR AgriMet public hourly responses retrieved 2026-10-19 UTC for the study window | `data/insitu_coverage/2026-10-19/` | Point values at nominal 5–5.08 cm; NRCS/AgriMet percent converted to m³/m³, NOAA already m³/m³; provider time labels normalized to UTC | [Validation workflow](../scripts/validate_eastern_washington.py) verifies cached responses, downloads only missing responses, applies the documented masks, matches acquisition times and records source URLs and hashes locally. |
| Station precipitation; optional jump diagnostic, excluded from masks and scores | NRCS hourly PRCP/PREC and NOAA USCRN P_CALC responses retrieved 2026-10-20–21 UTC | `data/insitu_precipitation/2026-10-20/` and the NOAA response above | Hourly precipitation in mm after source-unit conversion; UTC, preceding-hour support for NOAA P_CALC | Run [validation](../scripts/validate_eastern_washington.py) with `--with-precipitation --precip-cache-dir`; the resulting context does not change validation results. |
