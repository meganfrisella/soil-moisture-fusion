Initial Eastern Washington EarthScope/NRCS/NOAA/USGS catalogs: 32,802,158 response bytes downloaded in 136.081 s (0.230 MiB/s aggregate); verified raw responses and subsequent soil inventories are cached under `data/station_catalogs/2026-10-15/`. Activity is catalog-reported, not a live sensor check.

The additional soil inventory adds 65 markers inside the Eastern Washington WGS84 viewing box, for 75 total as of 2026-10-15; 42 are Cook Farm profiles rather than separate farms. AmeriFlux SWC lists are supplemented by published sensor evidence for [Hanford US-Hn1](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020JD032889) and [historical Cook, Lind, and Moses Lake towers](https://doi.org/10.1002/2017JG004148). The [national soil-moisture portal](https://www.drought.gov/drought-in-action/us-soil-moisture-networks-portal) cross-check returned the 10 NRCS/NOAA soil sites already mapped. Private Smart Farms sites and unconfirmed moisture sensors are excluded; archive end dates alone do not establish inactivity.
